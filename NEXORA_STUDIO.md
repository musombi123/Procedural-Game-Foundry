# NEXORA Interactive Studios

**Flagship product:** Nexora Foundry  
**Studio tagline:** Imagine beyond limits. Build worlds without boundaries.

NEXORA Interactive Studios is the independent studio identity behind original games, creator tools and experimental game-production technology. Nexora Foundry is its flagship model-free, procedural game creation platform.

## Product portfolio

- **Nexora Foundry** — creator-led pipeline for producing playable 3D games from authored briefs, consented player portraits and optional authorized video references.
- **Nexora Originals** — original games and intellectual properties developed by the studio.
- **Nexora Worlds** — reusable environments, characters, props and world-building assets.
- **Nexora Labs** — experimental gameplay systems and production technology.

## Studio boundaries

NEXORA is a separate venture and portfolio identity from RevelaCode. It must maintain distinct repositories, brand assets, product accounts, customer data, contracts, financial records and intellectual-property records. Any future licensing or technology-sharing arrangement with RevelaCode must be explicit and documented. No RevelaCode branding or SDA partnership claims should be added to Nexora products without authorization.

The name is a working brand selection, not a legal clearance. Trademark, company-name and domain availability must be checked before public commercial launch.

## Product principles

- Creator-authored stories and human approval at every production stage.
- Deterministic local tools and open-source production software; no paid inference APIs in the generation path.
- Consent and provenance for player likenesses and all supplied media.
- No claim of production readiness until a real creator run, human playtest, device validation and release gates pass.
