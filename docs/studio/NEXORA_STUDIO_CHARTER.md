# NEXORA Interactive Studios

**Studio identity and product charter**

*Imagine beyond limits. Build worlds without boundaries.*

## 1. Identity

- **Studio:** NEXORA Interactive Studios
- **Flagship product:** Nexora Foundry
- **Category:** Independent interactive entertainment and game-technology studio
- **Studio purpose:** Build original interactive experiences and practical technology that expands how games are created, played and shared.

NEXORA is the studio. Nexora Foundry is a product developed by the studio. Product names, repositories and interfaces must preserve that distinction.

## 2. Nexora Foundry

Nexora Foundry is a creator-led, local-first game production platform. Its intended workflow turns a creator-authored game brief and approved source assets into a staged, reviewable 3D game project.

The current engineering direction is deliberately tools-first:
- deterministic procedural generation;
- reusable asset and gameplay libraries;
- Blender for procedural 3D construction;
- Godot 4 for runtime assembly and validation;
- creator approval gates between production stages;
- no mandatory paid inference API or hosted generative-model dependency.

The platform must clearly distinguish implemented functionality, experimental functionality and planned work. A successful unit test or fixture-based runtime test is not proof that a creator's complete game has shipped.

## 3. Studio principles

1. **Creator authority:** the creator supplies the brief, owns creative decisions and approves each production stage.
2. **Rights and consent:** portrait, likeness, video, audio and third-party assets require appropriate rights and consent before processing or distribution.
3. **Reproducibility:** generation should be deterministic where practical, versioned and traceable to its inputs.
4. **Playable outcomes:** prioritize complete playable loops over decorative dashboards or empty feature surfaces.
5. **Honest readiness:** report toolchain availability, test coverage, known limitations and export status accurately.
6. **Open foundations:** prefer free and open-source tools where they meet the quality, license and performance requirements.
7. **Original IP:** NEXORA Originals and product-specific intellectual property must be tracked with clear authorship, licenses and provenance.

## 4. Independence from RevelaCode

NEXORA Interactive Studios is a separate venture and is not a RevelaCode hub, department or sub-brand.

Maintain separation across:
- studio identity, public communications and brand assets;
- repositories, project access and release credentials;
- intellectual-property records and third-party licenses;
- customer/player data, privacy notices and consent records;
- contracts, revenue, expenses and financial reporting.

Any future technology sharing, distribution, sponsorship or partnership with RevelaCode must be explicit and documented. No transfer of ownership or implied affiliation is created by shared tooling or personal founder involvement.

## 5. Product and IP portfolio

Nexora Foundry is the first confirmed flagship product. Additional games, tools, labels or studio divisions are not established by this charter; they require a deliberate product decision and their own scope, ownership and release plan.

## 6. Current engineering status

The Foundry repository contains a working engineering foundation and staged production subsystems. The assembly/runtime integration work remains subject to review and merge. A validated deterministic blockout is not equivalent to a finished commercial game, and CI fixture assets are not substitutes for testing real creator-provided assets.

Release claims must be supported by recorded end-to-end tests using actual generated assets, a creator-authored brief, Godot runtime validation and the relevant desktop or Android export toolchain.

## 7. Brand usage

Use **NEXORA Interactive Studios** for the studio and **Nexora Foundry** for the product. Do not use “NEXORA Foundry” as if it were the studio's legal name. Do not present a trademark, registered company, domain or legal clearance as established until independently verified.
